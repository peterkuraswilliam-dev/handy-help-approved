# Customer account and job journey

Status: future marketplace proposal.

Purpose: consolidate customer-specific expectations without duplicating feature details.

A customer should be able to maintain an account, submit a guided job with media, respond to admin questions, receive a small set of suitable quotes, choose a contractor, coordinate through protected channels, follow progress and review a completed job.

## Privacy defaults

- Unrelated customers and contractors cannot discover the account or jobs.
- Approximate location is used before selection where possible.
- Exact address/contact release requires an explicit authorised event.
- Customer deletion must account for active jobs, financial/audit/legal retention and reviews.

See [job posting](../features/job-posting.md), [matching](../features/matching-and-quotes.md) and [lifecycle](../features/job-lifecycle.md).
