# Founding members and influencers

Status: working proposal.

Purpose: preserve early-community concepts.

## Influencer application concept

Location, main platform, username/profile URL, follower count, content types, reason for joining and agreement. Proposed benefits included free founding status, badge, early access and local-business collaboration.

## Contractor founding concept

Early approved businesses join during the free development period and help test onboarding/marketplace operations.

## Open questions

Eligibility, approval criteria, safeguarding/advertising disclosures, permanent benefits, badge removal, data retention and whether follower count should influence acceptance. Location is required for genuine Aberdeenshire relevance.
