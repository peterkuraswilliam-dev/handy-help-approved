# Facebook group and contractor recruitment

Purpose: document the early community channel.

## Confirmed community rules

- The group supports Handy Help Aberdeenshire contractor recruitment and future customer awareness.
- Unapproved people may not officially accept jobs through the group/service.
- Every job requires admin approval.
- Participation is free while the application is being developed.
- Customers were not intended to require approval merely to ask for help; account/job verification rules remain future work.

Posts should distinguish community discussion from official approved jobs, state verification limits and route applicants privately rather than collecting sensitive documents in comments or Messenger.
