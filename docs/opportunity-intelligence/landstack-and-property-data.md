# Landstack and property data

Status: working research proposal; product capabilities/pricing require fresh verification.

## Landstack concept

Use planning/development and land/property intelligence to help contractors identify future work. Applicability to buildings/houses was discussed, but no integration was confirmed.

## Other potential signals

- Local planning authority open data
- New-build/development registers
- Registers of Scotland/recently sold property datasets or licensed alternatives
- Energy-performance/property attribute data where lawful
- Public tender/procurement notices

## Privacy and ethics

Use lawful data sources and licences; avoid publishing named homeowner targeting lists; respect direct-marketing rules; provide provenance/last-updated date; allow correction where appropriate. Prefer area-level opportunity insights over personal profiling.

## Open questions

Target contractor categories, data cost/licence, geographic coverage, update frequency, accuracy, lawful basis and whether leads are opportunities or verified customer jobs.
