# Launch and growth

Purpose: preserve the local go-to-market plan.

## Phase 1 — contractor supply

Recruit painters/decorators, gardeners, pressure washers, fencing/decking, electricians, builders/groundworkers, window cleaners, furniture assemblers, carpenters/joiners, plumbers, removals, outdoor cleaners, roofers and property maintenance. A landscaper was already available in an earlier discussion; exact live supply needs recheck.

Use direct local outreach, Google/Maps research, word of mouth, flyers/QR, the Facebook group and founder stories. Keep joining free during development and explain evidence requirements.

## Phase 2 — controlled customer pilot

Invite a limited geography/category cohort, manually approve every job, match by hand and measure time/quality. Do not outgrow admin capacity.

## Growth principles

Prove value before charging; publish clear verification limits; use authentic local case studies; avoid claims of guaranteed work. Expansion beyond Aberdeenshire requires an explicit decision.
