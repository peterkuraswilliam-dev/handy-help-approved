# Pricing history and options

Purpose: prevent old pricing ideas from being mistaken for confirmed plans.

## Historical visual — 5 August 2026

| Tier | Price | Illustrated benefits |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Starter | £10/month | Basic listing, profile, reviews, one lead/week |
| Pro | £25/month | Priority listing, unlimited leads, gallery, analytics |
| Premium | £50/month | Pro features, top placement, featured badge, visibility |

The same visual proposed 20 Pro businesses × £25 = £500/month. It also mentioned automation. This is **HISTORICAL**, not approved pricing; “priority/top placement” may conflict with fair matching.

## Current preferred direction

Free launch, then simple predictable contractor pricing and/or success fee; no lead credits. Candidate options:

1. One low monthly membership
2. Free basic membership plus transparent paid tools—not paid match ranking
3. Success fee on confirmed jobs
4. Hybrid capped monthly + success fee

## Decision needed

Validate willingness to pay, transaction/legal model, VAT/accounting and admin cost before publishing prices.
