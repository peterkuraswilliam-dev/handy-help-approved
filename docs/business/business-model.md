# Business model

Purpose: preserve business direction while keeping unresolved commercial choices explicit.

## Confirmed direction

- Local Aberdeenshire launch
- Free participation during the application-development/recruitment period
- Revenue target of £500/month
- Avoid pay-per-lead credits and bidding wars

## Preferred but unresolved model

Simple contractor monthly pricing and/or a success fee. The product should first prove genuine local jobs, fair matching and trust. Customer charges, deposits and platform payment remain open.

## Marketplace economics

Revenue must cover admin review, support, moderation, verification, notifications, hosting, payment costs (if used) and acquisition. Any paid priority must not weaken suitability, trust or equal access to safety-critical information.
