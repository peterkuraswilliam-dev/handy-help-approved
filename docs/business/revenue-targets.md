# Revenue targets

Purpose: turn the stated £500/month goal into testable scenarios, not forecasts.

| Scenario | Required volume | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| £25 monthly membership | 20 paying contractors | Historical reference scenario. |
| £15 monthly membership | 34 paying contractors | Rounds up; excludes churn/costs. |
| £5 success fee | 100 confirmed paid events/month | Commercial/legal feasibility open. |
| £10 success fee | 50 confirmed paid events/month | Requires reliable booking attribution. |

These are arithmetic scenarios, not demand forecasts. Track gross revenue, payment costs, refunds, acquisition, support time and churn before calling the goal sustainable.
