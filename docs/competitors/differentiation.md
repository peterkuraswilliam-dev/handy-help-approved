# Differentiation

Purpose: define how Handy Help should win without copying competitors wholesale.

## Defensible direction

- Real local admin in Aberdeenshire
- Approved contractors plus verified jobs
- Up to three suitable matches rather than broad lead resale
- Small jobs explicitly welcome
- Predictable pricing without lead credits
- Contact privacy until a legitimate relationship stage
- Clear lifecycle and Job Rescue/support

## Copy carefully

Clear standards/badges, strong local SEO, easy job forms, contractor galleries, transparent status and responsive communications.

## Avoid

Bidding wars, opaque ranking, excessive leads, misleading “vetted” claims, public contact leakage, pay-to-win suitability and automation that removes human accountability.
