# Development roadmap

Purpose: sequence evidence-backed work without promoting future concepts prematurely.

## Phase 1 — finish onboarding launch

1. Audit actual code/schema against docs.
2. Enforce owner/admin invitation-only contractor access and remove public contractor sign-up paths.
3. Complete contractor information-request/resubmission flow.
4. Complete approved profile/contractor management needed for launch.
5. Audit RLS, invitation tokens, storage, admin actions, sessions and transitions.
6. Add/verify audit trail and expiry management.
7. End-to-end and role testing; mobile/error polish.
8. Configure notifications later when provider is selected.
9. Launch invited contractor pilot.

## Phase 2 — validate marketplace

Resolve commercial/legal/contact-release questions; build customer job submission/admin moderation; manually curate limited matches and quotes; test lifecycle/reviews/support.

## Phase 3 — monetise and scale

Choose pricing/payment model from evidence; automate safe operations; add reporting, disputes and broader acquisition.

## Separate future track

Opportunity intelligence/land/property data after core marketplace validation.
