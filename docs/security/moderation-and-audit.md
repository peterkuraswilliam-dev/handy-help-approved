# Moderation, abuse prevention and audit

Purpose: connect human moderation with verifiable records.

Moderated objects include applications, jobs, profiles, portfolio/review media, messages/contact flags, reviews and disputes. Use explicit reason codes, free-text context, evidence references, actor/time, policy version and appeal status.

Abuse controls should cover fake jobs, spam, harassment, discriminatory requests, unsafe/illegal work, impersonation, review manipulation, repeated bypass attempts and admin misuse. Rate limits and automated signals support—not replace—human review for consequential decisions.

Audit events should be append-only to ordinary users/admins, avoid storing secrets/raw sensitive content and have restricted exports plus a defined retention policy.
