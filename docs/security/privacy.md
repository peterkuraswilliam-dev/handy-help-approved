# Privacy

Purpose: define privacy-by-design expectations.

Collect only data needed for approval, matching, safety and legal obligations. Tell users which fields become public. Keep precise addresses/contact/documents/private notes out of public projections, logs, analytics, URLs and notification bodies.

Document controller/processor roles, lawful bases, retention, user rights, recipients/providers, international transfers, cookies/analytics, incident handling and direct marketing before launch. Special care is required for identity evidence, allegations/disputes and inferred property/new-homeowner data.

See [contact protection](contact-protection.md) and [data retention](../database/data-retention.md).
