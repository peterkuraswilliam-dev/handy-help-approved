# Current scope

Purpose: define the safest evidence-backed implementation boundary.

## Current build slice — CONFIRMED

- Invitation-only contractor access: owner/admin secure-link creation, acceptance and revocation; no public contractor sign-up
- Contractor application and document submission
- Protected admin route and role-aware access
- Admin application queue, status filters, search and sorting
- Progress/warning/summary cards and loading/empty/error states
- Read-only application detail and activity
- Private notes, checklist and request-more-information workflow
- Approve, reject, suspend and restore actions with reasons/history
- Contractor response, missing information upload and resubmission
- Approved-contractor management and insurance-expiry handling
- RLS, document privacy, session/route protection and role testing
- End-to-end QA, mobile polish and launch preparation

## Explicitly outside the onboarding build

Customer accounts, job posting/job board, marketplace matching, payments/subscriptions, reviews/ratings, maps/directory, live chat, contracts, AI and advanced reporting.

These are not rejected product ideas; they are staged future scope. See [future scope](future-scope.md).
