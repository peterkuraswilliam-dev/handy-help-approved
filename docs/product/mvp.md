# Marketplace MVP proposal

Status: WORKING PROPOSAL

Purpose: describe the later managed marketplace without confusing it with the current onboarding build.

## Proposed MVP outcome

A customer submits a well-described job with photos. Admin verifies it. The platform selects up to three suitable approved contractors, who express interest or quote. The customer selects one; authorised contact/messaging becomes available; the job is scheduled, completed, confirmed and reviewed. Admin can intervene through complaints, suspension and Job Rescue.

## Proposed MVP capabilities

- Customer account and job submission
- Admin job moderation
- Category/location-based limited matching
- Contractor interest and quotes
- Selection/booking event
- Protected messaging/contact release
- Job lifecycle and evidence of completion
- Reviews tied to completed jobs
- Basic admin complaints and audit trail

Pricing and platform payment are not resolved and must not block a no-payment pilot unless the user confirms otherwise.
