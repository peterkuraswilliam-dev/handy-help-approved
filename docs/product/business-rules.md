# Business rules

Purpose: collect cross-feature rules; feature-specific detail remains in feature specs.

## Confirmed

1. Contractor access is invitation-only for the current launch; there is no public contractor sign-up.
2. Only the app owner or an authorised admin may invite a contractor through a secure sign-up link.
3. An invitation permits onboarding but does not approve the contractor.
4. Contractors cannot become approved without a separate owner/admin decision.
5. Every later marketplace job requires admin approval before it is posted or matched.
6. Unapproved people cannot accept work through the official community/service workflow.
7. Contact details must not be exchanged before the authorised booking/match point.
8. Contact details detected too early should be protected and the attempt flagged for admin review.
9. Early participation is free while the application is being developed.
10. Admin-only notes, invitation data and application documents are never public.

## Working proposals

1. Limit matching to up to three suitable contractors.
2. Allow small jobs and explicitly market them.
3. Tie reviews to completed/confirmed jobs.
4. Use Job Rescue for stalled or problematic jobs.
5. Use simple monthly pricing and/or success fees, not lead credits.
