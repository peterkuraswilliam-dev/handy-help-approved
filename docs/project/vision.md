# Vision

Purpose: record the intended long-term outcome without converting proposals into commitments.

## Confirmed intent

Build a trusted, local-first network that supports Aberdeenshire residents and local businesses. Human administration, transparent approval and privacy should remain visible parts of the service.

## Working proposition

“Local jobs. Trusted people. Real support.” Customers post once; Handy Help checks the job and connects them with a small number of suitable approved contractors. Contractors receive better-described, genuine opportunities without bidding wars or paid lead credits.

## Boundaries

Handy Help approval confirms supplied information and adherence to platform standards; it must not imply a guarantee of workmanship. Customers should still carry out appropriate checks. Legal wording requires review before launch.
