# Goals and measures

Purpose: separate real goals from illustrative dashboard numbers.

## Confirmed or explicit goals

- Launch the contractor onboarding and approval system safely.
- Recruit approved businesses covering a range of Aberdeenshire trades.
- Reach £500 monthly revenue after a free launch/development period.
- Build customer and contractor trust through verification and support.

## Proposed product measures

- Application completion and approval rate
- Median admin response time
- Information-request turnaround
- Insurance/document expiry compliance
- Verified job-to-match conversion
- Quote, booking and completion rates
- Complaint and Job Rescue rate
- Contractor/customer repeat usage

## Not verified

A 6 August 2026 project dashboard image showed 72% progress, 45/62 tasks, 620 estimated hours, 342 logged hours, £18,500/£24,000 budget and £45,000 estimated revenue. These are **illustrative/unverified**, not authoritative project records.
