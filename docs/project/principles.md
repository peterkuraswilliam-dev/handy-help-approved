# Product principles

Purpose: guide decisions where a detailed requirement is absent.

1. Local trust over marketplace volume.
2. Managed matching over an unmoderated directory.
3. Quality of opportunities over quantity of leads.
4. Small jobs are legitimate work.
5. Privacy by default; contact release only at an authorised stage.
6. Human review for consequential moderation decisions.
7. Clear states and reasons for every application/job decision.
8. Predictable pricing; avoid credit-based pay-per-lead mechanics.
9. Minimum necessary personal data and private documents.
10. Accessibility and mobile usability are launch requirements, not polish.
