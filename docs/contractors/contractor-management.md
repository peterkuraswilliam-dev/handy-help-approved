# Contractor management

Purpose: define post-approval admin operations.

## Current/follow-on capabilities

Approved contractor list, profile status, insurance/document expiry, reapproval, suspend/restore, performance notes and decision history.

## Rules

- Suspension immediately prevents new marketplace eligibility; public-profile behaviour is to be decided.
- Expiry does not silently delete history.
- Reapproval records new evidence and policy/checklist version.
- Performance notes are private and must not become an unexplained public score.
- Admin overrides require reasons and audit events.

Future job-based reliability signals require a transparent policy and appeal path.
