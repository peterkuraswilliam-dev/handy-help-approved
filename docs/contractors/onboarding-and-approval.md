# Contractor onboarding and approval operations

Purpose: operational companion to the [feature spec](../features/contractor-onboarding.md).

## Access gate — CONFIRMED

Contractor onboarding is invitation-only. The app owner or an authorised admin creates a secure sign-up link; there is no public contractor sign-up. Accepting an invitation allows the contractor to create their own account and apply, but does not confer approval or an Approved Contractor badge. See [invitation-only contractor access](../features/contractor-invitations.md).

## Evidence to collect — WORKING PROPOSAL

Business/sole-trader identity, contact details, services, areas, experience/about text, work examples, public liability insurance, relevant qualifications/certifications and agreement to community standards. Requirements must vary by service; do not imply every trade has the same legal obligations.

## Review controls

- Checklist version and reviewer
- Private notes separated from applicant-visible requests
- Mandatory reasons for rejection/suspension
- Decision history and evidence snapshot
- Expiry/renewal workflow
- Public-safe approval summary rather than raw documents

“Approved” means requested evidence was supplied and reviewed under the current policy. It is not a guarantee. See [security](../security/security-model.md) and [RLS](../database/rls.md).
