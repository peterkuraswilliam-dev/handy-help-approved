# Open questions

Purpose: central register of unresolved choices. Do not implement assumptions as answers.

## High priority

1. Is “MVP” currently the onboarding-only launch, or does it mean the later end-to-end customer marketplace?
2. What exact event releases contact details: mutual interest, customer selection, booking confirmation or payment?
3. What is the launch pricing model after the free period: one monthly plan, tiers, success fee, or a hybrid?
4. Will Handy Help take customer payments, deposits or only facilitate introductions?
5. What evidence is required for approval by trade/category, and how often is it rechecked?
6. What are the official application and job statuses and allowed transitions?
7. What is the legal scope of “approved”, “verified” and “Job Rescue” promises?

## Technical

8. Does the real repository use React SPA or Next.js, and is PWA support present?
9. What tables, policies, buckets and migrations already exist?
10. Which notification/email provider will be used?
11. How are admin and super-admin roles granted and reviewed?
12. What retention periods apply to rejected applications, identity/insurance files, messages and audit logs?
13. Is a postcode/radius model sufficient for service areas, or are polygons/settlement lists needed?
14. Should revoked or expired contractor invitations ever be restorable, or must an admin always create a replacement?

## Product/business

16. Can contractors publish public contact details on profiles, or must all contact remain protected?
17. Are contractor listings distinct from job listings in product language and navigation?
18. Are quotes binding estimates, messages, or structured offers with expiry/revision?
19. When may a review be submitted and can both sides review?
20. Who owns disputes and what remedies can admin offer?
21. What permanent benefits, if any, do founding contractors/influencers receive?
22. What geography follows Aberdeenshire and what evidence triggers expansion?
